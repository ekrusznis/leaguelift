# ADR-115 — Phase 37.7: reminder-toggle full wiring

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Context

Phase 28.2 (V67, ADR-088) built a real per-user, per-topic notification-preference system (`NotificationTopic` × `NotificationChannel` × `NotificationPreferenceState`, with a genuine SMS-consent gate) and a real resolver (`NotificationDeliveryResolver`) that folds all of it into one decision. But an audit for this slice found only two of the real send-paths actually call it — `BroadcastMessageService` and `ConversationMessageService` (both `NotificationTopic.MESSAGES`). Every other "reminder" path (`AnnouncementService` — which is what fee/document/eligibility/campaign reminders and general announcements actually route through, `EventRsvpService`'s staff notification) still resolved recipients off older, coarser mechanisms and never touched the preference table at all. The Settings page's topic toggles (Events & schedule, Fees & payments, Documents & eligibility, etc.) were real UI, real storage, real API — just not read by most of what they claim to control.

## Decision

**`AnnouncementService.publishInternal` now resolves every recipient through `NotificationDeliveryResolver`** — this is the single highest-leverage fix, since `AnnouncementKind` already covers five of the nine topics through one code path: `GENERAL → ANNOUNCEMENTS`, `CAMPAIGN_LAUNCH → FUNDRAISING`, `EVENT_REMINDER → EVENTS_SCHEDULE`, `FEE_REMINDER → FEES_PAYMENTS`, `DOCUMENT_REMINDER`/`ELIGIBILITY_REMINDER → DOCUMENTS_ELIGIBILITY`. `AnnouncementRecipientCandidate` already carried `userId`/`householdId` (real guardian-account resolution already existed at the SQL layer, in `AnnouncementRepository`'s guardian-listing queries) — the gap was purely that `publishInternal` never asked the resolver anything, just used the raw candidate email/phone gated only by the announcement's own `emailEnabled`/`smsEnabled` flags. Now each recipient's actual delivery (`inApp`/`email`/`sms`) comes from `NotificationDeliveryResolver.resolve(candidate.userId, candidate.householdId, topicFor(kind), candidate.email, candidate.phone)`, and the persisted recipient row reflects what was actually decided, not the raw candidate.

**`EventRsvpService.notifyStaffOfRsvpChange` now resolves each staff member's email through the same resolver**, topic `RSVP`. Team staff already have a real `userId` (no household ambiguity here, unlike family-facing paths), so this was a direct, low-risk wire-up.

**A real bug was caught by the test for this, not shipped**: the first draft wrote `notificationDeliveryResolver?.resolve(...)?.email ?: email` — chaining `?.email` after the nullable resolver call. This collapses two different things into the same fallback: "no resolver configured" (test-compat) and "resolver ran and explicitly decided email should be null" (a real disabled preference) both fall through to the raw `email`, silently defeating the entire point of the wiring. Fixed by computing the full `NotificationDeliveryDecision` first (falling back to a decision object, not a bare value, when the resolver is null) and then always reading `.email` off that decision. `AnnouncementService`'s implementation already used this correct shape from the start.

**`EventService.resolveFamilyRecipients` (event time/location/area/arrival-change notifications) and `FeePaymentReminderScanner` (fee-due reminders) are explicitly NOT wired in this slice.** Both resolve recipients at the household level only (`Household.contactEmail`/`contactPhone` + `emailRemindersOptOut`/`smsRemindersOptIn`), with no join to a specific guardian's `app_user` account — `NotificationDeliveryResolver.resolve` requires a `userId` to do anything beyond replicate existing behavior (a null `userId` is treated as "legacy contact, keep prior behavior" by design). Wiring these correctly means changing their recipient shape from "one contact per household" to "one contact per guardian" (mirroring the `household → household_adult → guardian_relationship → app_user` join `AnnouncementRepository`'s guardian-listing queries already use) — a real, larger data-shape change to `EventRecipientContact`/`FeePaymentReminderPayload` and their handlers, not a drop-in call. Both remain on the legacy household-level opt-out flags, which are real enforcement, just coarser-grained (whole household, not per-guardian, and not through the new preference UI) — this is a known, explicitly scoped-out gap, not a silent omission.

**`SponsorshipRenewalReminderHandler` stays untouched by design** — its recipient is a sponsor contact, not a Rally26 user; there's no account and no preference row to resolve.

**Verification:** ktlint clean, full backend suite green (940 tests, 2 new — one exercising the `AnnouncementService` topic-mapped resolver call and the DISABLED-preference-suppresses-delivery path, one exercising the `EventRsvpService` staff-email exclusion path and catching the `?.email ?:` bug above before it shipped).

## Consequences

- Every announcement/reminder sent through `AnnouncementService` (the majority of the codebase's "reminder" traffic) now genuinely respects the Settings page's per-topic toggles and SMS consent gate, closing the gap between what the UI promises and what the backend does.
- `EventService.resolveFamilyRecipients` and `FeePaymentReminderScanner` remain a real, tracked follow-up: migrating them to guardian-level resolution (same join pattern already proven in `AnnouncementRepository`) is the natural next step whenever that's prioritized, not a new investigation.
